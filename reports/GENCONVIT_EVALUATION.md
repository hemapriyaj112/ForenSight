# ForenSight — GenConViT Phase 2A Evaluation

**Date:** 2026-08-27
**Model:** GenConViT (official pretrained video deepfake detector, MIT license)
**Scope:** Independent measurement of GenConViT as a standalone video signal.
GenConViT is **NOT** fused into the production verdict (`config.genconvit.use_in_final_fusion=false`).
No thresholds or fusion weights were changed.

Source of truth: `reports/testing_videos_evaluation.json` (generated by
`py scripts/evaluate_testing_videos.py`).

---

## 1. What was measured

- **Production verdict** (texture heuristic + provenance override + audio) — the
  current shipped signal, used as the baseline.
- **GenConViT standalone** — the neural detector's own `genconvit_predicted_label`
  (FAKE/REAL) against folder-derived ground truth, on videos where it produced a
  score (a face crop was found in at least one sampled frame).
- **Disagreement table** — where GenConViT and the texture heuristic disagree.

## 2. Results — production verdict (untouched baseline)

Ground truth: 11 FAKE / 4 REAL.

| metric | value |
|---|---|
| TP / TN / FP / FN | 6 / 4 / 0 / 5 |
| Accuracy | 0.667 |
| Precision (FAKE) | 1.000 |
| Recall / FAKE_RECALL | 0.545 |
| F1 (FAKE) | 0.706 |
| False-negative rate | 0.455 |
| False-positive rate | 0.000 |

> This matches the Phase 1 baseline exactly (TP=6, TN=4, FP=0, FN=5,
> FAKE_RECALL=0.545), confirming that wiring GenConViT in with
> `use_in_final_fusion=false` did **not** perturb the production signal.

## 3. Results — GenConViT standalone

11 videos scored (9 FAKE / 2 REAL); 3 FAKE and 1 REAL video had no face crop in the
sampled frames and produced no GenConViT score.

| metric | value |
|---|---|
| Videos scored | 11 |
| TP / TN / FP / FN | 3 / 1 / 1 / 6 |
| Accuracy | 0.364 |
| Precision (FAKE) | 0.750 |
| Recall / FAKE_RECALL | 0.333 |
| F1 (FAKE) | 0.462 |
| False-negative rate | 0.667 |
| False-positive rate | 0.500 |
| Real-rejection rate | 0.500 |
| FAKE verdict rate | 0.364 |

**Interpretation.** Standalone GenConViT is **weaker than the texture heuristic** on
this small corpus: FAKE recall 0.333 vs 0.545, and it produced a false positive on a
REAL video. However it is genuinely complementary on hard examples — see §4.

## 4. Disagreement vs the texture heuristic

| filename | ground truth | GenConViT | texture | verdict | genconvit frames |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ai_vid_without_audio.mp4 | FAKE | REAL | UNCERTAIN | FAKE | 1 |

| girl_speaking video gemini.mp4 | FAKE | REAL | FAKE | FAKE | ? |
| insta vid.mp4 | FAKE | REAL | UNCERTAIN | REAL | ? |
| real vid ai aud.mp4 | FAKE | **FAKE** | REAL | REAL | ? |
| south indian women speaking.mp4 | FAKE | **FAKE** | FAKE | FAKE | ? |
| WhatsApp …6.15.45 PM.mp4 | REAL | **FAKE** | UNCERTAIN | REAL | 1 |
| WhatsApp …6.15.46 PM.mp4 | REAL | REAL | REAL | REAL | ? |

Highlights:
- **Complementary catch:** `real vid ai aud.mp4` (FAKE) — GenConViT says FAKE while
  texture says REAL; the current verdict (REAL) is a missed fake that GenConViT
  would have caught.
- **Possible over-flag:** `WhatsApp …6.15.45 PM.mp4` (REAL) — GenConViT said FAKE,
  but it scored off a **single face crop** (frames=1) and also on a compressed
  WhatsApp re-encode, so this is a weak-evidence false positive.
- **Single-frame caveat:** several videos (`ai_vid_without_audio`, `crackers_withaud`,
  `WhatsApp …6.15.45`) were scored from only **one** detectable face frame because
  MTCNN found no face in the other sampled frames. A 1-frame GenConViT score is far
  less reliable than the 15-frame default the model was designed for.

## 5. Frames per video

Videos where `genconvit_frames_used == 1` should be treated as low-confidence:
`ai_vid_without_audio.mp4`, `crackers_withaud.mp4`,
`WhatsApp Video 2026-07-24 at 6.15.45 PM.mp4`. The remainder used the sampled
multi-frame path (default `num_frames: 15`).

## 6. Conclusion / recommendation

- GenConViT is a **real, free, locally-run neural detector** that now loads and runs
  with zero missing weights (ED + VAE branches).
- It is **not** better than the texture heuristic on this corpus **standalone**
  (FAKE recall 0.333 < 0.545; FPR 0.5 > 0).
- It is **complementary**: it uniquely flags `real vid ai aud.mp4`, and on videos
  where the production path already relies on provenance override it frequently
  points the same way.
- **Recommendation:** KEEP as a **secondary signal** — surface `genconvit_score` /
  `genconvit_predicted_label` in results (already done) but **do not** enable
  `use_in_final_fusion` until its real-rejection rate is improved / face coverage
  widens. See `reports/PHASE2A_GENCONVIT_REPORT.md`.
